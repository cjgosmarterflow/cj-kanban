**What was done:**

Completed a full audit of both GHL sub-accounts and built five smart lists on each to give us an accurate, ongoing view of the entire contact database.

**Smart lists built (live on both Christina and Josh):**

- **Excl — All Exclusions** — catches every contact that must never receive SMS: anyone tagged dnc, stop, wrong number, not interested, or error, plus anyone with SMS Do Not Disturb enabled. Josh: 5,674 contacts. Christina: 5,444 contacts.
- **Active — Untouched** — contacts currently active in the cold outreach phase. Requires the untouched tag, the AddedToNew2026 tag, a valid phone number, first name, and last name, and excludes everyone in the exclusions list. Josh: 13,336 contacts. Christina: 5,682 contacts.
- **Active — Interested / Warm** — contacts currently in the warm nurture phase. Has the interested tag or the needs follow-up workflow tag, and is not excluded. Josh: 2,576 contacts. Christina: 5,829 contacts.
- **Eligible — Not Yet in Any Workflow** — clean contacts who have never been enrolled in any phase, are not excluded, and are safe to enroll immediately. Josh: 262 contacts. Christina: 478 contacts.
- **Stuck — Never Enrolled** — contacts who have the AddedToNew2026 trigger tag but never entered any workflow, are not excluded, and are not in an active phase. Josh: 76 contacts. Christina: 8,993 contacts.

**The big finding — Christina's 8,993 stuck contacts:**

GHL workflow triggers only fire when a tag is added to a contact, not when the tag already exists. These 8,993 contacts had the AddedToNew2026 tag before the new workflows were built, so the trigger never fired for them. They are reachable, not opted out, and ready to enroll — they just need the tag re-added via bulk action.

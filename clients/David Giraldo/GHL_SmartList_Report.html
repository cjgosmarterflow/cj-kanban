How We Found the 8,993
Stuck Contacts — Step by Step
Step 1: 24,106 contacts have the tag added to new 2026 workflow — nearly the entire database.

Step 2: Filtered for contacts with this tag but without untouched — meaning never enrolled into the active workflow. Result: 18,225.

Step 3: Removed contacts with exclusion tags (dnc, stop, wrong number, not interested, error, DND) — these cannot be contacted. Result: 14,657.

Step 4: Removed contacts already in an active workflow (interested, needs follow-up workflow) — already being worked. Final count: 8,993 contacts ready to enroll.

Why are they stuck? The workflow trigger fires only when a tag is added. These contacts already had the tag before the new workflows were built, so the trigger never fired for them.

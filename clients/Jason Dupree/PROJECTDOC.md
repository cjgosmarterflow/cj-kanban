## Scope

### Phase 1 — Funnel (3 pages)
- **Landing Page** — hero video + product copy, CTA to book consultation
- **Booking Page** — GHL calendar embed, auto confirmation email + SMS
- **Thank You Page** — booking confirmation, sets expectation for consultation

### Phase 2 — Automations
- Appointment reminders (24hr + 1hr before)
- No-show → reschedule sequence
- Post-consultation branches:
  - **Closed** → Loan Processing → Client Success → Referral Campaign → Long-Term Relationship
  - **Needs Time** → Short-term follow-up → Long-Term Nurture
  - **Credit Repair** → Referral to credit repair partner → Monthly Progress Check → re-book
  - **Not Interested** → Newsletter/Market Updates → Reactivation Campaign

### Phase 3 — Real Estate Agent Track (separate segment)
- SMS blast campaign to RE agents (A2P verification required first)
- Partner meeting → Active Referral Partner → Partner Nurture flow
- Different follow-up logic than consumer track

## Voice AI Design (Non-Negotiable Rules)

- **4 separate agents** — one per campaign. Do NOT combine into one agent.
- **Per-call flow:** intro → explain relevant loan programs → qualify → live transfer to licensed broker / book callback / send SMS+email → CRM disposition → escalate to human on request
- **Outbound trigger:** GHL native "Voice AI Outbound Call" action inside a workflow
- **Caller ID:** One phone number per campaign (from-number set in workflow action)
- **GHL verified limits (June 2026):** 1,000 calls/day per location, 10 calls/min, 8AM–8PM contact timezone. Each number called once/day, max 14x in 14 days.
- **Requires:** KYC verification + outbound enabled on location. Business responsible for TCPA consent.
